# Sales-Data-Analysis
Sales data analysis project using SQL, Excel and Power BI to clean data, analyze sales performance, and track revenue and product trends.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to understand revenue, profit, product performance, city-wise sales, category profitability, and monthly sales trends.

The project demonstrates the use of SQL, Excel, and Power BI to perform data analysis and create an interactive business dashboard.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Calculate revenue, profit, and profit margin
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare sales performance across cities
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Compare profitability across product categories
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard

## Key Insights

- Total revenue is approximately ₹1.9M.
- Total profit is approximately ₹1.6M.
- The dataset contains 50 orders and 397 units sold.
- Overall profit margin is approximately 85.6%.
- Laptop is the highest-revenue product in the analysis.
- Mumbai is the highest-revenue city.
- Electronics is the most profitable category.
- Monthly revenue varies considerably throughout the year, with noticeable peaks during the year.
